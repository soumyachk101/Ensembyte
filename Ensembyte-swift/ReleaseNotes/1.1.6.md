Ensembyte 1.1.6 — by Soumya Chakraborty. Universal Release for macOS, Windows & Linux.

## New features

### Precision Effort Slider & Controls
- **Modern Rectangular Effort Track**: Overhauled the effort slider with a sleek 28pt rounded-rect track layout (`RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius: 8)`).
- **Pearl-White Elevated Puck**: Replaced the circular glass lens with an elevated pearl-white sliding puck (`RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius: 6)`), featuring a highlight border, drop shadow, center grip notch, spring drag feedback, and a dynamic electric aura glow at Max effort.
- **Dynamic Reasoning Tier Gradients**: Implemented dedicated palettes for each reasoning tier: zinc/gray (Low), sky blue (Medium), indigo (High), rich violet (Extra High), and electric multi-gradient (Max).
- **Interactive Step Dots & Pop Animation**: Added 4px step markers with spring pop haptics (`1.85x`) on step transition and automatic smooth fade-out at Max effort.
- **Extended Thinking Integration**: Integrated the extended thinking toggle directly within the composer effort slider card.

### Fluid Thinking & Reasoning Block
- **Thinking Shader Orb**: Continuously rotating and pulsing multi-gradient shader orb (cyan, violet, fuchsia, blue) during active reasoning.
- **Live Elapsed Timer**: Real-time present-tense status indicator ("Thinking…") with live second counter.
- **Auto-Collapsing Summary Badge**: Automatically collapses completed thoughts into a badge ("Thought for 12s") with expandable trail, vertical gradient guide line, and markdown view.

## Refinements

- **Vertical Spacing Alignment**: Clean separation between the effort track and the "Recommended" indicator marker to prevent any visual overlap.
- **Max Effort Sweeping Sheen**: Replaced particle noise with a smooth, glossy light sheen wave across the Max effort gradient track.
- **Right Edge Gap Elimination**: Ensured full edge-to-edge gradient coverage on the track at Max effort.
